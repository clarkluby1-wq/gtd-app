# Release Notes

What's changed in the GTD app, in plain language — newest first.

---

## September 22, 2026

**Sync between devices**
Sign in from Settings to keep the same tasks and projects on your computer and your phone. Works offline too, and catches back up once you're online again.

**A phone-friendly layout**
A new menu bar across the bottom makes it easy to jump between Today, Inbox, Next Actions, Projects and everything else with your thumb. Buttons like the star and delete are now easy to tap on a touchscreen, not just reachable with a mouse.

**A new "Today" screen**
Your day at a glance: today's Short List, what's on your calendar, and a one-tap way into your guided morning routine.

**Short List, extended**
You can now flag a Waiting For item or something on your Calendar as part of today's Short List — not just Next Actions.
